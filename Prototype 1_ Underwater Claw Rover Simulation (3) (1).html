Prototype 1: underwater claw rover

Old smartphone + LED ring (vision)
Proximity sensor
Two-joint arm and claw
Continuous tracks (left and right)
Rod linking the arm to the net lid
Onboard storage net
It searches in a stop-and-scan pattern, locks on, drives up, grips the waste and swings it over the net. A rod from the arm opens the net lid only at that moment, so no sensor is needed. Lower the water clarity to see the proximity sensor take over.
